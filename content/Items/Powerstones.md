Powerstones are items necessary to transport back to Earth after falling into a [[Rift]]. They come in several grades, with the current known grades being F-tier and E-tier. According to the Crown System, they can also be used in crafting potions and items.

- F-tier: Transport 1 person back to earth.
- E-tier: Transport 4 people back to earth.

# Natural veins
It appears to be possible for veins of Powerstones to occur naturally, as seen in the area surrounding [[The Sao Paolo Rift]]

# Powerstone experiments
Things that can be attempted with Powerstones:
- Create "escape gates" while on Earth.
- Give the powerstone to a member of a Crafting class and have them attempt to craft an item.
