A pendant shaped like a moon, found in the [[Ant Cave]]. It contains a vow dedicated to [[Jana]], some of the effects of which expired on its owner's death.

It is possible to feel a pull to the other half of the amulet.
- Pointing North-east (from the [[Ant Cave]])
- Pointing South-east (from the [[City of Imortis]], the signal is noticeably fainter)
