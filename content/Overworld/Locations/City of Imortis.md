A desolate city, filled with undead and necromancers. It used to be part of a larger continent, but is currently free-floating and cut off from other places. The city is divided into several tiers.

As they progress upwards, the districts become fancier, but also more well-protected with stronger and more alert undead.

## Important people
### Iseah Museaos
[[Iseah]] is the "rightful ruler" of the City of Imortis (according to herself), and is currently fighting to take it back from the undead. She is the only living person we have encountered in Imortis, and claims she hasn't seen another living person in ages either.

### High Priest
The High Priest lives in the [[City of Imortis#Keep]]. He used to be the High Priest of [[Xoros]], but he became corrupted and rules it as an undead monstrosity now. He is able to "assume control" of undead in the city.

### Nobles
The old nobles of the City before its undead takeover. They betrayed the city and cut a deal with the High Priest to become his minions in exchange for power.

## Creatures
### Ghoul
Ghouls are servants of the necromancers, digging out and fetching corpses.

### Bloated Zombie
A type of zombie that runs at you and explodes.

### Zombie
A type of weaker undead, but hard to kill.

## Features
### Lower City
The lower city is the least-protected part of the city, and undead often aren't very strong or sentient here, unless the High Priest chooses to assume direct control of them.

#### Chapel of Xoros
An abandoned gothic-looking holy temple to [[Xoros]]. The area around this temple actually has faint signs of life, such as a few sprigs of grass struggling to grow out of the dead soil.

Inside the temple are stained glass windows depicting various landscapes, including beautiful fields but also areas that look hellish. There is also one stained-glass window of [[Xoros]] himself, holding a quill and writing.

The temple is covered in a thick layer of dust, and one corpse of a follower of Xoros is hidden in one of the alcoves, clutching a silver holy symbol of a quill. Despite being covered in a thick layer of dust, the corpse is amazingly well-preserved as if it died mere minutes ago. It appears to have died from a stab wound. Some old footsteps are visible in the dust, leading to the corpse.

#### Bone Market
A hub for necromancers to buy body parts for their undead experiments, positioned in the large abandoned skeleton of a Dragon.

The place from which the High Priest rules the City.

#### Pyramids
Two pyramids and a ziggurat at the center of the city. The [[City of Imortis#Nobles]] live here.

## Star
In the sky above Imortis, a pale green astronomical object is visible, which could be a large star or moon of some kind.
